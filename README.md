# PKmon Card Vault

Scan, grade, price and track a Pokémon TCG collection in your phone's browser.

**Open it:** https://nk7ghub.github.io/pkmon/

- Scan cards with the camera or from photos (several cards per photo works). Each card is matched against a local index of every card by number, set size, name, HP and attack names. When the match is uncertain, you pick from a grid of the best matches.
- Pre-grade condition: centering is measured from the border widths; corners, edges and surface are estimated from the image.
- Prices: TCGplayer and Cardmarket via pokemontcg.io and TCGdex, plus eBay sold links. Graded values are model estimates.
- Portfolio dashboard with a 30-day/1-year trend, sealed products, master-set progress and 33 currencies.
- Trade analyzer, CSV/text/PDF/JSON export, and shareable binder links.

Everything runs in the browser; your collection is stored on your device. See `handoff.md` for how it works and the change log.
